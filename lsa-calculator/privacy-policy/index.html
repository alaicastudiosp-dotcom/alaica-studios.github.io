LSA Calculator Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

AlaiCa Studios develops LSA Calculator. Contact us about this policy or privacy requests at alaicastudiosp@gmail.com.

Information entered and stored

The app accepts names (including another person’s name for comparisons), deployment dates, LSA levels, accumulated qualifying days, payroll cut-off adjustments and optional rank/name text for share cards. Saved calculations include relevant inputs, calculation mode, save date, record ID, estimated totals, qualifying and calendar days, rate breakdowns, monthly earnings and estimated payment dates. These are estimates, not bank-account or payslip records. The app also stores free-comparison usage.

Local storage, backups and exports

Saved calculations are stored locally using device preferences. The app does not automatically upload calculation records to an Alaica Studios server. Depending on your device and account settings, Android backup may include these preferences. You may choose to export/import JSON records, save or print PDFs, and save or share comparison images. Files you export or share may be stored by cloud services or recipients you select. Exported JSON is not encrypted by the app.

Purchases and Pro

Google Play handles payment transactions. RevenueCat processes purchase history, a pseudonymous app user ID and entitlement information to verify and restore Pro access and for purchase-related analytics. The app does not send names or deployment details from calculations as RevenueCat customer attributes. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy.

Advertising

The app integrates Google Mobile Ads. The advertising SDK may process IP address, advertising/app-set identifiers where available, app interactions and diagnostic data for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. SDK processing may occur even if a Pro user does not see a banner. The calculator does not request precise GPS location. See Google’s advertising information.

Accounts

No LSA Calculator account, registration or password is required to calculate LSA. Google Play purchases use your store account. The inspected app does not separately integrate Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics.

Deletion and your choices

You can delete individual saved calculations and choose whether to export, import or share records. Deleting an in-app record does not remove exported/shared copies, device backups or third-party purchase records. Contact us at alaicastudiosp@gmail.com for privacy questions or deletion assistance; requests involving store transaction records may need to be handled by the relevant provider.

Retention and security

Local records remain until deleted or app data is cleared, subject to device backup settings. Protect any exported files containing names or dates. Third-party providers apply their own security and retention practices.

Audience and independent status

The app is intended for people planning military allowance estimates and is not designed for children. LSA Calculator is independently developed and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or officially connected to the UK Ministry of Defence or HM Armed Forces. Figures are estimates, not official pay advice.

Policy changes

We may update this policy as the app, service providers or requirements change. We will revise the effective date above when we publish an update.
